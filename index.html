STATUTORY CITIZEN'S CHARTER

Municipal Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

Every grievance lodged on GeoTagged is bound by statutory time limits under the Urban Local Bodies Redressal Act. Issues not resolved within the SLA are automatically escalated.

🧹

Solid Waste & Garbage

Health & Sanitation Wing

Overflowing bins, uncollected road waste, and illegal dumping.

⚡ SLA: 24 Hours Escalation: Health Officer
🚰

Water Supply & Leakage

Water & Sewerage Board

Pipeline bursts, contaminated water supply, and valve failures.

⚡ SLA: 24 Hours Escalation: Assistant Exec Engineer
💡

Street Lighting & Electrical

Electrical Maintenance Division

Non-functional streetlights, open junction boxes, damaged poles.

⏱ SLA: 48 Hours Escalation: Zonal Engineer
🛣

Road Repairs & Potholes

Public Works Dept (PWD)

Potholes, broken footpaths, road cave-ins, and uneven surfaces.

⏱ SLA: 7 Working Days Escalation: Executive Engineer (Roads)
🌊

Drainage & Stormwater

Storm Water Drainage Wing

Clogged storm drains, waterlogging, broken manhole covers.

⏱ SLA: 48 Hours Escalation: Superintendent Engineer
🌳

Public Spaces & Other

Town Planning & Horticulture

Fallen tree branches, park maintenance, encroachment notices.

⏱ SLA: 5 Working Days Escalation: Joint Commissioner
